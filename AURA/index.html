The Origin

A obsession with
clean beauty.

In 2019, founder Léa Moreau was sitting in her grandmother's rose garden in Grasse, France — the perfume capital of the world. She held a single rosebud, marveling at how something so delicate could hold such profound complexity.

She had spent twelve years in cosmetic chemistry, growing increasingly uncomfortable with the synthetic compounds her industry relied on. "I knew what went into those beautiful jars," she says. "And I knew it didn't need to be that way."

AURA launched six months later with three products and an unwavering belief: your skin deserves the same care a gardener gives their most precious bloom — patient, intentional, and deeply rooted in nature.
