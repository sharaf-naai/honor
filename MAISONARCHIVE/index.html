The Living City

مراكش
Marrakech

Step inside the world that births every object in our archive — ancient, sensory, and eternally alive

I
The Medina

A labyrinth unchanged since the 12th century. 26 km of winding alleys where craftspeople still work in family ateliers, the scent of cedar dust and leather tanning carried on the air.

II
The Souks

Organised by trade since the Almoravid era. The blacksmiths here, the dyers there. A living museum of commerce where every object has a lineage and negotiation is an art form.

III
The Zellige

Hand-chipped geometric tiles laid by masters called maâlems. Each pattern — the eight-pointed star, the interlocking hexagon — encodes centuries of mathematical beauty.

IV
The Riad

An inward-facing house built around a courtyard and fountain. The outside world is shut away; inside is perfume, silence, and the sound of water — the original luxury.

The Art
of Making

Four stages from medina to home — nothing skipped, nothing rushed
01
Source & Select

We travel through Morocco's souks to identify craftspeople whose techniques meet our exacting standards.

02
Design & Dialogue

Working alongside artisans, we develop pieces that honour tradition while speaking to contemporary living.

03
Craft & Wait

Each object is made entirely by hand. Time is an ingredient. We do not set production quotas.

04
Archive & Deliver

Each piece arrives with full provenance documentation, its maker's mark, and a certificate of authenticity.
