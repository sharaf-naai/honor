02 / About Us

The Studio
Behind the Space

Sophie Laurent
Founder & Principal Designer

We believe that architecture is the most intimate form of storytelling — it shapes how people wake up, how they gather, how they rest. Lumina was founded on the conviction that every space deserves to be extraordinary.

Established in 2012, Lumina has grown from a two-person studio into an internationally recognized practice working across residential, hospitality, and cultural sectors. What hasn't changed is our approach: slow, deliberate, deeply personal.

We don't follow trends. We study light. We study how people move. We study the materials that will age with the building and become more beautiful over time. The result is spaces that feel inevitable — as if they could not have been any other way.

Each project begins with listening and ends with something that lasts well beyond the final walk-through.

Craft
We work with artisans and makers who share our commitment to quality over speed. Every finish has a reason.
Restraint
We believe subtraction is harder than addition. We remove until what remains is essential.
Longevity
Materials chosen to age gracefully. Spaces designed to grow with the people who inhabit them.
Dialogue
Great design is a conversation. We listen first, draw second, and always leave room to revise.

04 / Expertise

What We Do
& How We Do It

Our Services

We offer a complete design service — from the first conversation to the final object placed in the room. Each service can stand alone or be combined into an end-to-end engagement.

01
Consultation
Before we design anything, we listen. Our consultation service is a focused engagement to understand your goals, clarify what's possible within your budget, and establish a clear direction for your project.
  • Site or space analysis
  • Mood board and visual direction
  • Budget and timeline framework
  • Scope of work document
02
Architecture
Full architectural services from schematic design through construction administration. We work with residential, boutique commercial, and mixed-use typologies — always with an emphasis on material quality and spatial experience.
  • Schematic and design development
  • Construction documents
  • Permit coordination
  • Construction administration
03
Interior Design
We design interiors that feel composed rather than decorated — spaces where materials, light, and furniture exist in dialogue with one another. Our process is collaborative and methodical, with an eye for the precise and the rare.
  • Space planning and layout
  • Material and finish specifications
  • Custom furniture and millwork design
  • Procurement and installation management
04
Art Direction
The final layer of a space: the objects, art, and atmospheres that give it a soul. We source and curate pieces that are meaningful, not merely aesthetic — working with galleries, artists, and craftspeople worldwide.
  • Art curation and commissioning
  • Object and textile sourcing
  • Scent, sound, and light atmosphere
  • Photography and documentation

Our Process

Step 01
Discovery
We listen to your story, your needs, your life — before a single concept is formed.
Step 02
Concept
A clear design direction presented through sketches, references, and materials.
Step 03
Design
Detailed drawings, specifications, and selections developed in close collaboration.
Step 04
Execution
We oversee every contractor and craftsperson to ensure the vision is realized faithfully.
Step 05
Handover
Final styling, photography, and a space that is ready to be lived in from day one.
