About

DRESSED in intention, NOT TRENDS

Fashion, to me, is not what you wear — it is what you choose to say without opening your mouth. Every silhouette, every texture, every shade holds a sentence. I am obsessed with the story a garment tells when it enters a room before the person wearing it does.

This portfolio is a living archive of editorials, model collaborations and looks that explore fashion as identity, politics, and poetry.
